## What it is

The bedside screen's main view: where today stands right now. It's minimal on purpose, low-light and read from the parent chair.

- **Home:** how she is right now (one large line), a link to today's updates ("Today · 3 updates" with the latest), the next care time, and today's forming star. Today's moments drift into the star and add light; tonight it joins her sky (Sara's view of past days).
- **Two styles** (switch with "Night Nursery / Confetti" in the prototype bar), from the design board (https://claude.ai/artifact/PxFGyjAc6utRRLH3f1A1zt). Both on a black sky, all stars, firsts as planets:
  - **Night Nursery:** Nimesh's look. Cream starlight, copper warmth for a parent, violet gas, Instrument Serif + Geist.
  - **Confetti Constellations:** playful. Chunky colorful stars, thick light-blue paper lines, confetti and grain, Young Serif + Figtree.
- **Home** (Home C; A and B were dropped): today's sky. The day is an arc across the whole screen, drawn like the v2 night sky: warm sparkles for changes, small stars for care times, soft rings for team decisions, faint twinkling background stars. Today's star glows at "now"; **dragging it back along the arc** (or the arrow keys) goes back through the day and snaps to moments, with a few soft words underneath. Family moments come from her nurse's notes at the bedside and have a small warm light on the arc. **"What's ahead ›"** sits at the end of the arc, where the day runs out; tapping it (or the dashed future) opens What's ahead.
  - **Her progress** (bottom left) opens the day view with Sara's baby drawing and the line of stars to scrub, at the same moment the star is on.
  - **Her galaxy** (bottom left, or pinch out over the sky) zooms out: today's arc shrinks into its star, then her whole stay opens around it, drawn the way Nimesh's galaxy works (`explorations/nimesh/galaxy`): day 1 at the center, days spiraling out, each week its own constellation; a star grows with her weight, simplifies as she needs less breathing help, glows warm when a parent was there, and is dimmer on a hard day. It's drawn here in the chosen style; a link opens Nimesh's live galaxy (port 3000) too. "‹ Today" zooms back in.
- **Her sky** ("Her galaxy" on home, or pinch out): her whole stay, zoomable the way Nimesh's galaxy works, in both styles. Days spiral out from day 1; each week is its own constellation. One camera flies between levels: **galaxy** (W1–W4 and Today on hairline labels) → **week** (the week framed on the left; its story, firsts and "What was hard" on the right) → **day** (her star in a 24-hour ring, midnight at the top: family and firsts nearest, care times on the ring, changes outside, a "now" hand on today) → **moment** (tap one to read it in plain words). Back goes up a level, ← / → move between weeks or days, Esc steps out. **Drag** (or two-finger scroll) to look around, **pinch** to zoom, swipe sideways on a day for the day before or after; "Center" flies back. Space is drawn in depth layers (far dust drifts slowly, near motes are soft and pass faster), so moving feels like moving through space. Weeks are named in plain labels ("Week 2 · Finding a rhythm"); both styles use colored stars so neighboring days are easy to tell apart.
- **What's ahead** (with Back): her care plans, kept separate from her sky but drawn in the same gentle way. Each area her team is working on (Breathing, Feeding, Staying warm) is a short path of stars: lit for steps reached, glowing for the step she's on, an outline for what's next, with the plan in one line and a soft status ("Decided", "Still being decided", "For now"). Beside it: what's coming up and checks waiting on a date. No going-home dates; a step back reads as "working on this again".
- **Today so far** (full page, with Back next to her name). It keeps home's header and the "Contact care team" button in the same place, so the change of page doesn't jump, but has its own layout:
  - **Left: the day as a short list**, one line each, in time order with a "Now" marker: what changed, team decisions (tagged Decided or Deciding), next care (glowing), later, and anything waiting on a date. Routine care is one quiet line underneath.
  - **Right: the selected item, shown visually.** A wire change shows on her body (Sara's scan) with a short plain explanation; a decision shows as a card with its status and where it came from; next care shows the time large, what a parent can do, and why care is grouped.
  - Text appears only for the item a parent picks, so the page never reads like a chart.
- **Family moments** (a parent present, joining a care time, reading to her) come from what her nurse notes in her chart. Only the monitor is in scope; there is no phone app.
- **Discharge (first pass, Sara):** "Time to discharge!" (top right of Mila's stars) first retells her stay in her sky (day 1 with her size at birth, then each first and each change in her care, the hard day too, while her constellations fill in week by week; auto-advances, tap or → to go on, "Skip to mobile" / "Replay"), then her stars lift and fly onto a 3D baby mobile (three.js, `src/current/Discharge.jsx`). Each week's constellation, with the same star drawings, positions and lines as her sky, hangs on a thin string from a ring, in week order. It turns slowly, each strand sways on its own, and you can drag to turn it; "‹ Mila's stars" goes back.
- **Contact care team:** shows her team right now, with one "Contact Priya" action for her nurse (the charge nurse covers if she's busy). That opens an optional note with three gentle sample prompts; "Ask for help" sends it, with or without a note, and the sheet confirms Priya has been contacted and reminds parents that in an emergency they should press the nurse call button by her bed.

Night mode dims everything to the time, one line, the next care and the star. The bar above the screen is for the prototype only: day vs. night, and the two design systems (DS1, DS2).

Open questions:
- Nimesh's galaxy uses its own synthetic baby (Arjun, a 54-day stay) while this view uses Mila on day 23. The team should pick one dataset before converging in `app/`.
- The brief said no touch, for infection control. The link and the button need a wipeable touch screen, or could move to the parent's phone.
- My copy of Sara's scan now draws a CPAP mask and can show her setup at any time of day (`present` and `changed`); worth folding back into Sara's version if the team keeps it.

## Why we tried it

To join the two ideas into one story: the bedside view shows today as it forms, and Sara's sky keeps each finished day as a memory.

## Run it

```bash
cd explorations/jayden/current-view
npm install
npm run dev
```

Open the address Vite prints. `?view=sky` opens straight to the sky.
