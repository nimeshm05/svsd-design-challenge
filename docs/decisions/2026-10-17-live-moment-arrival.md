# The live moment arrival

**Question:** When a new moment is logged (a 2 PM feed), how should it reach the parent?

**Chose:** A mote of light travels in from the top-right edge of the screen (where the notification will appear), arcs along a curve, slows as it nears today's star and is absorbed. The star answers with a ring ripple and a brief swell of its core, and a small toast says "A new moment · Arjun finished his 2 PM feed" with "See it". If the parent is already looking at today, the mote lands on the moment's place on the 24-hour ring and the new moment flashes in.

**Why:** It turns a log entry into something that *arrives*, the closest thing to being there. The movement is slow (3.2 s) and lands softly, so a notification never feels like an alarm. With reduced motion it takes 0.6 s.

**Rejected:** a badge count or red dot. That's the language of things you owe, not of your child.
