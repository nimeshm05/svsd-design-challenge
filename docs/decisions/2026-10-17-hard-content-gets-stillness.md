# Hard days get stillness, not effects

**Question:** How should the prototype show a setback (week 3: back on CPAP)?

**Chose:**
- The app never breaks bad news first. The day's moment says the care team called, and when.
- The week has a quiet "What was hard" section: plain context (how common this is, what the extra support is for), no icons, no colour change, no animation beyond the standard fade.
- Hard-day stars are dimmer and cooler, but have the same spikes, halos and presence as any other day.
- Copy avoids "fighter" or "warrior" language: the baby is growing, not battling.

**Why:** Research says the biggest stressors are how the baby looks and behaves, and losing the parent role. Drama on a hard day makes both worse. Stillness reads as respect.

**Open:** the dataset and copy assume the baby is doing well by week 8. We still need a path for a baby who doesn't go home, and none of the code may assume a happy ending.
