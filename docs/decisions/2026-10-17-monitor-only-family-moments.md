# Monitor only: family moments come from the bedside, not a phone

**Question.** The bedside display shows family moments (a parent reading to her, being there for a care time). Earlier explorations had parents save these on a phone app. Where should they come from?

**Options considered**
1. A parent phone app where parents save moments (and see her sky away from the hospital).
2. A check-in on the bedside screen ("I'm here").
3. What her nurse already notes in her chart: a parent present, joining a care time, reading or singing to her.

**What we chose.** Option 3, and the monitor is the only surface in scope. The phone version is out of scope for this prototype.

**Why**
- One surface to design and demo well by Monday; a second app would split the work.
- Nurses already chart family presence and participation in many NICUs, so the moments are realistic and need no extra work from parents. We avoided moments no one would record (for example, "Mom stayed with her through the night").
- The copy names the source ("Noted by Marcus, her night nurse"), so parents know where it came from.
- It keeps the tone we want: the screen tells the story of her day, including the people who were with her, without asking parents to log anything or making absence feel like a gap.
