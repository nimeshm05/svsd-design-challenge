# Merging Sara's current view into Jayden's bedside display

**Question.** Sara forked Jayden's current view and built on it in parallel. Where both changed the same part, whose version goes into the bedside-display story?

**Options considered, per overlap.** Keep Jayden's, take Sara's, or blend.

**What we chose.** Jayden decided each overlap:

| Part | Chosen | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Care button | Sara's "Contact care team" flow | Shows who is caring for her and lets parents add a note, with urgent help still at the bedside call button. |
| What's ahead | Sara's Timeline and Next Steps tabs | Easier to scan by day and by step. |
| Medical terms | Sara's tappable glossary | Real clinical words, explained when tapped. |
| Her sky (chapter 4) | Sara's in-app zigzag galaxy | Weeks read in order, with a caption for each. |
| Going home (chapter 5) | Sara's Discharge view (baby mobile) | Her sky becomes something the family takes home. |
| Baby | Mila | Matches every other screen in the story. |

Kept from Jayden's: Story / Bedside, the six chapters, her first day, who she is, her 3D outline, night view, today becoming a star, and chapter 6 ("If she doesn't go home").
